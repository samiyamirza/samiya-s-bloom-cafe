Our Story

Grown with love, served with a smile

Little Bloom began as a flower stand on a sunny street corner. Samiya dreamed of a place where a good cup of tea and a bunch of fresh flowers could share the same counter — and so this cafe was born.

Everything here is made in small batches: syrups infused with real petals, bread proofed overnight, and cakes decorated like little gardens.

  • 🌼 Fresh daily bakes
  • 🌹 Seasonal flower shop
  • 🍵 House-blend teas
“Every cup should bloom a little joy in your day.” — Samiya, founder

Our little story

About Our Boutique 🌸

Hello, I'm Samiya Mirza — the flower lover and cake baker behind Samiya's Little Bloom Cafe. What began in 2018 as a tiny table of home-grown flowers has blossomed into a dreamy little boutique where fresh blooms share a shelf with warm cupcakes and fudgy brownies.

Everything here is made in small batches with love — flowers hand-tied every morning, cakes baked fresh daily, and handmade keychains crocheted stitch by stitch. We're a small luxury boutique, not a big supermarket: one table, two passions, endless love. 💖
